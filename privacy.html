Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Second Brain is a personal tool used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's own Google Drive, Gmail and Google Calendar solely to store files the owner sends, read emails the owner labels for filing, and read and add events to the owner's calendars.

  • Data is stored only in the owner's own Google account and the owner's private repository.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No other users' data is collected.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
